# Kitchenware
Stainless Steel Kitchenware
Stainless steel kitchenware is durable, but that doesn't mean every piece leaving a factory will be perfect.

Small manufacturing defects can affect appearance, usability, and safety.

Surface Problems

Inspect for:

Scratches
Dents
Uneven polishing
Discoloration
Stains

A consistent surface finish is particularly important for retail products where appearance affects customer perception.

Sharp Edges

Edges should be smooth and safe to handle.

Burrs or poorly finished edges can turn a seemingly minor manufacturing issue into a safety problem.

Inspection guidance for stainless steel kitchen utensils commonly includes checking smooth surfaces, burrs, welding areas, dimensions, and structural strength.

Handles and Connections

If the product has a handle, pay attention to the connection point.

Check whether it is:

Secure
Properly aligned
Smoothly finished
Resistant to normal use
Material Verification

Don't assume that every product described as "stainless steel" uses the exact grade you requested.

For projects where the material grade matters, request appropriate documentation or testing.
